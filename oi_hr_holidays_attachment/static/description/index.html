Attachment Required Leave Settings

"From Submission to Approval, Track Your Leave Processes"

This module helps companies and organizations manage their employee leave requests more efficiently.

Description

The module requires employees to attach supporting documents or files, such as medical certificates, vacation plans, or other relevant documents, when submitting their leave requests. With this module, employers can set up specific attachment requirements for different types of leave requests, such as sick, vacation, or maternity leave. The module also allows employers to track the status of each employee's leave request, from submission to approval or rejection.

This means that employers can easily keep track of all leave requests from submission to approval or rejection, ensuring that they are always up-to-date with their staff's leave schedules. The "Leave Request" module is a simple yet powerful tool that can help streamline the leave request process, ensuring that employees receive their entitled leave and employers can manage their workforce efficiently.
